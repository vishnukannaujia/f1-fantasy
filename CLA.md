## How to sign

Until an automated CLA check is set up, sign by adding a line to
`CONTRIBUTORS.md` in your pull request:

```
- <Your Name> <email> — I have read and agree to the CLA (YYYY-MM-DD)
```

Corporate contributors (submitting on behalf of an employer) should have someone
authorized to bind the company agree on the company's behalf.
